# Google Form field guide

Create two separate forms. One submission represents one complete team and must be completed by the captain or manager.

## Shared instruction block

Show this at the top of both forms:

> Only the team captain or team manager must complete this form. One form submission represents one complete team. Your team is not officially registered immediately after submitting this form. Registration is confirmed only after payment verification by the tournament administration. Never upload fake, edited, cancelled, failed, or old payment screenshots.

## Football form

- Team Information: Team Name, City / Area, Jersey Primary Colour, Jersey Secondary Colour, Team Captain Name, Captain Mobile Number, Captain WhatsApp Number, Captain Email, Alternative Contact Number, Coach or Manager Name, Team Logo (optional file upload).
- Players 1–7 required; players 8–12 optional. Each player has Name, Jersey Number, Position (Goalkeeper / Defender / Midfielder / Forward). Mark Team Captain and Goalkeeper.
- Payment: payer name, payer mobile, amount paid, payment date, approximate payment time, payment app, UPI Transaction ID / UTR, payment screenshot (one image/PDF up to 10 MB), and the payment declaration.
- Rules: accept tournament rules, confirm player details, understand submission is not confirmation, understand payment verification is required, opt into updates, and acknowledge false/duplicate payment details may be rejected.

## Cricket form

- Team Information: use the same shared team fields.
- Players 1–11 required; players 12–15 optional. Each player has Name, Jersey Number, Role (Batter / Bowler / All-Rounder / Wicketkeeper / Wicketkeeper-Batter). Mark Captain, Vice Captain, and Primary Wicketkeeper.
- Payment and rules: use the same required evidence and declarations.

## Confirmation message

```text
Your team registration has been submitted successfully.

Your payment details are currently under verification.
Your team is not yet officially confirmed.

The tournament administration will verify the payment and send confirmation through WhatsApp or phone.
Please save your UPI Transaction ID for reference.
```

Never use “Payment Successful” in the form confirmation message.
